{# ---------------- KNN ---------------- #}

2. K-Nearest Neighbors — yield estimate

Used on Grains & Plants
Predicted cropCurrent NDVI + groundwater →Find 3 most similar past seasons →Average their yield

Measured with leave-one-out testing: every real season is hidden in turn, its yield is estimated from its 3 nearest neighbours, and the estimate is compared with what was actually harvested.
